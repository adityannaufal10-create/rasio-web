# Approved GRAIN UI direction

Audience: competition judges watching a presentation and interacting with the prototype.

Scope: GRAIN only. Preserve every analytical control, route, dataset, scientific estimate, SVG download, keyboard shortcut, and country/cluster interaction. Existing local research edits remain intact.

Composition: spacious editorial landing, geographic focal object, linked module strip, retained spatial narrative, scientific evidence, retained six-part workflow, live instrument previews, efficient workspace navigation.

Appearance: light/dark/system. Silver and graphite supporting surfaces, calm blue primary actions, mist blue selection, muted burgundy in comparative data graphics. Scientific cluster/heatmap colors keep their identity.

Cards and KPIs: thin border, 12px corners, no decorative glow, circular 40px neutral icon, medium-weight title, generous but purposeful spacing, quiet metadata, tabular numerals. Containers adapt to screen width; tables retain horizontal scrolling inside their own region.

Motion: visible defaults; short panel/dialog responses; frame-rate-independent globe motion; offscreen rendering suppressed; reduced motion supported. Small/short viewports use explicit narrative phase controls instead of oversized pinned stages.

Verification: production build, five representative viewport widths in both themes across all six routes, text contrast/overflow/asset checks, and interactive smoke checks. Screenshots are evidence only for their recorded dimensions; physical device testing is outside the completed browser audit.
