# GRAIN

GRAIN is Team IRIS's RASIO 10.0 research prototype for explaining Asia-Pacific agrifood emissions and demonstrating regional policy analysis to competition judges.

The landing page presents the research story. The workspace supports cartography and five clusters, spatial econometrics, policy simulation, methane forecasting, and methodology. Country audit, scientific figure inspection/download, four map layers, global search, keyboard shortcuts, and the presentation path remain available.

Preserve scientific data, formulas, estimates, content, and existing public figure assets. UI work must not silently revise research claims. The separate `prototype caliber` application is outside this redesign.

The approved direction combines Apple Store composition with an efficient workspace. Supporting UI uses graphite, silver, mist blue, and muted burgundy; analytic category colors retain their meanings. Light, dark, and system appearances are supported. Cards follow the user's reference: thin borders, restrained rounding, circular neutral icons, medium-weight headings, and readable descriptions.
